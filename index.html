Connection Method

Method: STUN direct.

Accepted values
  • STUN direct: uses the built-in STUN servers. Leave URL, username, and credential empty.
  • TURN fallback: needs a TURN URL. It tries direct routes first, then the relay.
  • TURN relay only: needs a TURN URL. It forces all traffic through the relay.
  • URL: must start with turn: or turns:, for example turn:relay.example.com:3478, turn:relay.example.com:3478?transport=udp, turn:relay.example.com:3478?transport=tcp, or turns:relay.example.com:5349.
  • Username: paste the exact username from your TURN provider. Leave empty only if the provider says no username is required.
  • Credential: paste the exact password, token, or secret from your TURN provider. Leave empty only if the provider says no credential is required.
